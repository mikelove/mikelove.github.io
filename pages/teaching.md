### Mentorship <a href="#mentorship" title="Link to this section" style="text-decoration:none; color:#ccc;">#</a>

Below is information for students interested in mentorship or joining
the lab.

<a id="student-categories"></a>
#### Information by student category <a href="#student-categories" title="Link to this section" style="text-decoration:none; color:#ccc;">#</a>

* **Prospective PhD students:** please apply through BBSP or the
  Biostatistics department (see links below). You can mention your
  interest in the Love lab in your application; we do not need to meet
  beforehand for you to express interest in the lab. I generally will
  not reply to emails about joining the lab from prospective students.
* **Current UNC graduate students:** you are welcome to email me
  about rotations, courses, etc.
* **UNC undergraduates:** spots in the lab are limited, but some years
  I have capacity to supervise honors theses.
* **Visiting graduate students from outside UNC:** these are
  considered case-by-case.
* **High school students and undergraduates from other universities:**
  unfortunately I cannot provide mentorship to high school students or
  undergraduate students from other universities.

<a id="graduate-programs"></a>
#### Graduate programs at UNC <a href="#graduate-programs" title="Link to this section" style="text-decoration:none; color:#ccc;">#</a>

I am part of the leadership of the Bioinformatics and Computational Biology (BCB) PhD program within the Biological & Biomedical Sciences Program (BBSP) in the School of Medicine of UNC-Chapel Hill. Note that there is not direct application or admittance into labs/programs: _"Students are admitted into the BBSP for the first year of graduate study. During this year you are free to explore departments, programs, labs and courses before committing to a specific thesis lab or PhD program."_

Links about BCB and BBSP:

* [UNC BCB](https://bcb.unc.edu/)
* [UNC BBSP](https://bbsp.unc.edu/)
* [How to apply to BBSP](https://bbsp.unc.edu/prospective-students/apply/) and [BCB application notes](https://bcb.unc.edu/admissions/)

Basic questions about the BCB and BBSP programs are likely answered
in the informational links above. I may not reply to emails unless
they are about information not present in these links.

I am also a faculty in the Biostatistics Department at the Gillings School of Public Health:

* [UNC Biostatistics](https://sph.unc.edu/bios/biostatistics/)
* [How to apply to Biostatistics](https://sph.unc.edu/bios/bios-how-to-apply/)
